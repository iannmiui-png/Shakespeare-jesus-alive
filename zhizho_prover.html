intonation and confession in 27-EDO — the decoder hears only the audio

the lattice · one column per edon, one row per voice
the text to be intonated
what the machine heard

Intonate renders the text as chordal trytes — one tribble per voice, Dominant/Mediant/Tonic at 1760/880/440 Hz, one 27-EDO step per edon, with the Diatonic a step above — and plays the buffer. Confess runs a Goertzel bank over that same rendered audio and reassembles characters from pitch alone; the round trip is exact when the verdict says so. Listen at the door opens the microphone and decodes the loudest stable chord in the room, quarter-second by quarter-second — sing to it, or play it a recording of itself.
